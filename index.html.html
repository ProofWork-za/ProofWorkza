How it works

Six steps from
potential to proof.

Skills Assessment

Complete a 20-minute skills and readiness assessment. We evaluate your strengths and digital capabilities to ensure every match is the right one.

Step 02

Matched to a business based on your skills, interests, and location. Every project has defined deliverables, a clear timeline, and a completion fee.

Step 03

Complete a structured 4–6 week project for a real business. Real brief, real deliverables, real impact — not simulated experience in a classroom.

Businesses hire participants full-time, offer further projects, or recommend them across their network. Your proof opens doors that a CV never could.
